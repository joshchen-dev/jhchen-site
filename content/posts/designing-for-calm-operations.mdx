Reliable software is not software that never fails. It is software whose failure modes are understood, visible, and recoverable.

## Start with the operator

Before adding another dashboard, ask what decision an engineer needs to make at 3 a.m. A useful signal should narrow the search space and point toward an action.

## Prefer boring recovery paths

Runbooks, bounded retries, graceful degradation, and rehearsed rollback procedures rarely look exciting. During an incident, that is exactly their strength.

The best operational systems turn uncertainty into a short sequence of well-understood choices.
